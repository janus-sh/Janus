# Code of conduct

Be kind. Assume good intent. Criticize code, not people.

Not welcome here: harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexual remarks aimed at people, and publishing someone's private information.

Jokes are fine. Jokes at someone's expense are not.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, use **Report content** in the menu next to the comment.
